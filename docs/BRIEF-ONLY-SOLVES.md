# Brief-only solves of the v2 jobs

**Why.** The person who writes a job's checks also writes its reference, so choices that are not in the brief can quietly become hidden requirements. Before a job is frozen, a solver that has never seen its checks or reference solves it from the brief and starter files alone. Every failure counts as a brief or check bug until shown otherwise.

## How (docs/PLAN-V2.md section 2.3, point 9)

A fresh session of the builder's model family, in a scratch folder holding only the ticket and the
room, told not to read the repository, writes its result as `briefonly/calls.json` (replayed through the real
agent, so its finish claim is graded) and a note of where it found every answer. A job rests on the room only if
the solve needs no assumption that changes a graded answer.
