Title: Kestria: central bank preview
Date: 2026-11-02
Desk: Rates

Consensus expects the Kestrian central bank to cut its policy rate by 25bp to 3.00% on Thursday. We think it will hold: core inflation is still above target, and the economy grew faster than expected in the third quarter.

Pension fund demand for long-dated bonds should pick up as funding levels improve. Inflation-linked bonds look fairly priced against our inflation outlook. The term structure of volatility slopes upward: the market fears the new year more than the old. Sentiment toward long-dated bonds softened over the month as pension funds digested the path of interest rates.
