Title: Valdoria: third-quarter GDP preview
Date: 2026-10-27
Desk: Macro

The first estimate of Valdoria's third-quarter GDP is published on Friday. We forecast growth of 0.5% quarter on quarter, with household spending the main driver and net trade a drag after September's jump in imports.

The risk is to the downside: the PMI dipped below 50 in September, and inventories may have subtracted from growth.

We see the risks to activity as balanced in the near term and tilted to the downside next year. Business surveys have become a less reliable guide to output, so we put more weight on hard data. Net trade has been volatile from month to month, so we look at the trend over a quarter.
