Title: Bellamy Foods: third-quarter trading update
Date: 2026-11-04
Desk: Equities

Bellamy Foods reported third-quarter revenue of EUR 1,421m, 3.2% more than a year earlier, with price increases making up for slightly lower volumes.

It lowered its operating profit guidance for 2026 to EUR 385m to EUR 425m, citing cocoa costs and weaker sales in its snacks business. The cut is smaller than we feared.

We would use any weakness after results to add to our preferred names. Companies with high fixed costs have the most to gain from a modest pick-up in demand. Earnings revisions have turned less negative, and the season so far has delivered more beats than misses.
