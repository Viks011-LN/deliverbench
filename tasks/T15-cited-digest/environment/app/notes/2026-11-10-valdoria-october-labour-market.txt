Title: Valdoria: October labour market
Date: 2026-11-10
Desk: Macro

The unemployment rate edged down to 5.1% in October. Employment growth slowed to 9,000 on the month, and wage growth to 3.9% year on year.

Markets have looked past freight costs more than the activity data in recent weeks. Services inflation is proving stickier than goods inflation, which has all but stopped rising. Household spending has held up better than confidence surveys suggested, helped by real wage gains.
