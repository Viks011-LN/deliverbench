Title: Kestria: house prices in September
Date: 2026-10-07
Desk: Macro

Kestrian house prices rose 0.6% in September, and are 3.9% higher than a year ago. Mortgage approvals rose for a third month as rates came down.

Supply remains tight: listings are well below their long-run average, which supports prices even as demand cools.

Survey evidence points to a broad-based recovery in services, while manufacturing remains the weak spot. The housing market is responding to lower mortgage rates more slowly than in past cycles. Household spending has held up better than confidence surveys suggested, helped by real wage gains.
