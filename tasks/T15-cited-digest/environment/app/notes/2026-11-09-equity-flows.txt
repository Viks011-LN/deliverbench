Title: Equity flows
Date: 2026-11-09
Desk: Strategy

The equity volatility index ended the fortnight at 15.1. Two-year swap rates in the major markets moved by 13bp on average over the fortnight. The regional equity index slipped 0.6% on the month, with chemicals the best performer.

The backdrop is one of slowing but positive growth and inflation drifting lower. The quality factor has had its best quarter in two years, helped by falling bond volatility. We would rather add risk on a pullback than chase the recent rally. Small companies remain cheap against large ones, but they need lower rates to close the gap.

Dividend cover is comfortable for most of the companies we follow. Working capital has been a drag on cash flow this year as companies rebuilt stocks. Results days have brought larger share-price moves than usual, a sign that positioning is crowded.
