Title: Valdoria: September labour market
Date: 2026-10-02
Desk: Macro

The unemployment rate fell to 5.2% in September from 5.3% in August, the lowest reading since early 2024. Employment rose by 18,000 on the month, led by services; manufacturing payrolls were flat.

Wage growth eased to 4.1% year on year. Vacancies fell for a fourth month, so the labour market is cooling from the demand side rather than through job losses.

Consumer credit growth has picked up, mainly in card spending. Markets have weighed the outlook for consumer spending more than the activity data in recent weeks. Household spending has held up better than confidence surveys suggested, helped by real wage gains.
