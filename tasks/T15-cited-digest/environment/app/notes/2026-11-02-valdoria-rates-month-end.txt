Title: Valdoria rates: October month-end
Date: 2026-11-02
Desk: Rates

The Valdorian 10-year government bond yield ended October at 3.42%, up from 3.18% at the end of September. Curve steepening was the theme of the month as term premia rose.

Two-year yields were little changed, anchored by the central bank's guidance.

The market is pricing a gentler path of cuts than the central banks' own projections imply. Front-end pricing has swung between one and two further moves, and is likely to keep doing so. Liquidity in the long end remains thin, and moves tend to overshoot on light volumes. We prefer to hold steepeners in the five-to-thirty-year sector.
