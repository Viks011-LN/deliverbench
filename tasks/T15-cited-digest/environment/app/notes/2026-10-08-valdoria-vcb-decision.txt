Title: Valdoria: the VCB raises its deposit rate
Date: 2026-10-08
Desk: Rates

The Valdorian Central Bank raised its deposit rate by 25bp to 3.75% at its October meeting, as most economists had expected. Two of the seven board members voted to hold.

The statement kept the door open to a further move but stressed that decisions will depend on the data, and on services inflation above all.

Supply is the main near-term risk for longer maturities, with heavy issuance scheduled before the year end. Cross-market spreads have narrowed as investors look for yield across the region. We would fade any move that relies on one data release alone.
