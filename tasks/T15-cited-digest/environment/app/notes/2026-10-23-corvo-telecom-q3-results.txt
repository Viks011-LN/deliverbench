Title: Corvo Telecom: third-quarter results
Date: 2026-10-23
Desk: Equities

Corvo Telecom, Valdoria's incumbent operator, reported third-quarter EBITDA of EUR 496m on revenue of EUR 1,284m, ahead of expectations. Mobile service revenue grew 3% on a year earlier, while fixed-line revenue was flat.

The company raised its guidance for the 2026 EBITDA margin to 38.0% to 39.5%, and cut its capital expenditure guidance to EUR 620m to EUR 650m as network spending comes in below plan.

Management teams have become more cautious about the second half in their outlook statements. Guidance has been conservative this season, which leaves room for upgrades in the new year. Earnings revisions have turned less negative, and the season so far has delivered more beats than misses. Input-cost relief from lower freight and energy is now flowing through to gross margins.
