Title: The week ahead
Date: 2026-11-09
Desk: Strategy

Tuesday: Valdoria's October labour market. Wednesday: the third estimate of Kestria's third-quarter GDP, and Pellmark Retail's third-quarter trading update. Thursday: Valdoria's October trade, with revisions to September.

Kestria's October consumer prices are published on Monday 16 November, and we will send our preview on Thursday.

The backdrop is one of slowing but positive growth and inflation drifting lower. Earnings, not multiples, will have to do the work next year. We keep a small overweight in credit, funded from government bonds.

Firms report that hiring intentions have cooled, although layoffs remain rare. Firms' price expectations have fallen to their lowest level in two years.
