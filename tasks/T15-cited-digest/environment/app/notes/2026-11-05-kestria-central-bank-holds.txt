Title: Kestria: the central bank holds
Date: 2026-11-05
Desk: Rates

The Kestrian central bank kept its policy rate at 3.25% on Thursday, against a consensus that had expected a cut. The vote was five to two.

The statement pointed to core inflation and to the strength of the economy in the third quarter. We expect the first cut in the spring.

The term structure of volatility slopes upward: the market fears the new year more than the old. Short-dated bonds have been anchored by the policy outlook, while the long end has traded on supply. Curve steepening has been the theme of the week as term premia rose across the region.
