Title: Norrland Industrial: third-quarter results
Date: 2026-10-22
Desk: Equities

Norrland Industrial reported third-quarter revenue of EUR 4,216m, 5.9% higher than a year earlier, and an adjusted EBITA margin of 12.4%. Order intake was EUR 4.6bn, helped by two large rail contracts.

Management kept its revenue guidance for 2026 at EUR 16.5bn to EUR 17.0bn and will update investors on its longer-term plans at the capital markets day on 5 November.

Free cash flow was strong, and net debt fell. The service business grew fastest, which helps the margin.

Guidance has been conservative this season, which leaves room for upgrades in the new year. Earnings revisions have turned less negative, and the season so far has delivered more beats than misses. Cost programmes announced last year are now showing up in the margins. Currency moves have been a smaller drag on reported growth than a year ago.
