Title: Kestria: third-quarter GDP, flash estimate
Date: 2026-10-14
Desk: Macro

Kestria's economy grew 0.9% quarter on quarter in the third quarter, according to the flash estimate, ahead of the 0.8% consensus. Investment in renewable energy contributed most of the gain.

Household spending was flat. The second estimate, with the breakdown by sector, follows at the end of the month.
