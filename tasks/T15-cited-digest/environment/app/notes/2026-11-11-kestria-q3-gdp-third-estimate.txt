Title: Kestria: third-quarter GDP, third estimate
Date: 2026-11-11
Desk: Macro

Kestria's third-quarter growth was revised down to 1.0 per cent quarter on quarter in the third estimate, from 1.1 per cent in the second, as investment was marked lower.

This is the last estimate before next year's annual benchmark revision. The Kestrian economy is still growing faster than its neighbour's.
