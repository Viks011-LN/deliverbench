Title: Kestria: October inflation preview
Date: 2026-11-12
Desk: Macro

Kestria's consumer prices for October are published on Monday 16 November. We forecast inflation of 2.6% year on year, up from 2.4% in September, as last autumn's fall in energy prices drops out of the comparison.

The consensus of economists for October inflation is 2.5%. A figure above the consensus would make a cut in December less likely.
