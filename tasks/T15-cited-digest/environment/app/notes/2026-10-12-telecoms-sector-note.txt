Title: Telecoms: subscribers and pricing
Date: 2026-10-12
Desk: Equities

Price increases have stuck across the region's mobile markets, and churn has not risen as feared. Subscriber numbers at the end of June:

Operator        Country   Mobile subscribers (thousands)  Change on a year earlier
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Corvo Telecom   Valdoria  6,412                           +1.8%
Corvus Telecom  Kestria   3,177                           +3.5%
Valdonet        Valdoria  4,026                           -0.9%

Corvo Telecom, Valdoria's incumbent operator, has gained subscribers despite raising prices twice. Corvus Telecom, Kestria's second mobile operator, is growing faster from a smaller base.

We prefer companies with net cash and pricing power over those that rely on volume growth. Companies with high fixed costs have the most to gain from a modest pick-up in demand. Sentiment toward real estate firmed over the week as retail investors digested the year-end budget round. Input-cost relief from lower freight and energy is now flowing through to gross margins. Consolidation remains a theme in the sector, though regulators have become stricter.

Spectrum

Valdoria's spectrum auction in November is the next test of discipline. We expect the incumbent to bid for capacity rather than coverage.

Working capital has been a drag on cash flow this year as companies rebuilt stocks. Labour costs remain the biggest unknown for next year's margins. The sector trades at a premium to its history because of the visibility of its earnings. Results days have brought larger share-price moves than usual, a sign that positioning is crowded.
