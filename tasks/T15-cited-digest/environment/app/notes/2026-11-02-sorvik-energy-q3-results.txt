Title: Sorvik Energy: third-quarter results
Date: 2026-11-02
Desk: Equities

Sorvik Energy's third-quarter revenue rose to EUR 3,113m, while its operating margin fell to 13.9% as maintenance at two fields cut volumes and lifted unit costs.

The company cut its capital expenditure guidance for 2026 to EUR 1.5bn to EUR 1.9bn, deferring one project to next year. Net debt was stable.

Producers have kept spending in check, which limits the supply response to higher prices. Forward curves have flattened, signalling a better-supplied market next year. Stocks held by consumers of industrial metals are low, which supports prices on any recovery.

Labour costs remain the biggest unknown for next year's margins. We would use any weakness after results to add to our preferred names. The sector trades at a premium to its history because of the visibility of its earnings.
