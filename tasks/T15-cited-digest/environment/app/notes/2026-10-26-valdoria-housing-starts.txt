Title: Valdoria: September housing starts
Date: 2026-10-26
Desk: Macro

Housing starts rose to 12,450 units in September from 11,980 in August. Permits, a leading indicator, edged lower, so we expect starts to soften into the winter.

Real incomes are rising again, which should support consumption into the new year. Firms' price expectations have fallen to their lowest level in two years.
