Title: Credit flows: the first week of the quarter
Date: 2026-10-05
Desk: Credit

The new quarter opened with a busy primary market, as issuers moved before results blackouts.

Secondary liquidity is adequate, though bid-offer spreads widen quickly on risk-off days. Rating agencies have become more willing to look through one-off costs. We prefer industrial and utility paper over financials at current levels. Primary markets were busy, with issuers bringing deals forward ahead of results blackouts. Fallen-angel risk is concentrated in a handful of consumer names with weak cash flow. Bank loan markets have reopened for mid-sized companies, which takes pressure off bond issuance.

The average new-issue premium was 7bp. Investment-grade spreads ended the quarter so far at 105bp, and high-yield spreads at 370bp.

Issuance from Valdorian industrials was light, and we expect Norrland Industrial to come to market after its results rather than before.

Covenant quality in new high-yield deals has deteriorated again. We see better value in the five-to-seven-year part of the curve than in the long end. Hybrid issuance has picked up as issuers lock in funding before rates fall further.
