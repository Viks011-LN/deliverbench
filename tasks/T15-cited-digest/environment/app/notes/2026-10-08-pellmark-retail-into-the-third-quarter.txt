Title: Pellmark Retail: into the third quarter
Date: 2026-10-08
Desk: Equities

Pellmark Retail, the Valdorian discount retailer, told investors on Thursday that trading in the third quarter had started in line with its plans. Second-quarter sales were EUR 2.61bn, and the company guides to like-for-like sales growth of 2% to 4% for 2026.

First-half gross margin was 38.2%, against 37.6% a year earlier, as the company bought better and cut markdowns. Management expects a smaller gain in the second half as it invests in prices.

Pricing power is the theme of the season: companies that held prices have protected their margins. Working capital has been a drag on cash flow this year as companies rebuilt stocks. Capital spending plans are rising for the first time in two years. Currency moves have been a smaller drag on reported growth than a year ago.

We keep our positive view. The sale of the Kestrian stores, agreed in June, removes a loss-making business and simplifies the story.
