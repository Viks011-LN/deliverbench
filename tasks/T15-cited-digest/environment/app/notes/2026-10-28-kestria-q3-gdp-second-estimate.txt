Title: Kestria: third-quarter GDP, second estimate
Date: 2026-10-28
Desk: Macro

Kestria's third-quarter growth was revised up to 1.1% quarter on quarter in the second estimate, from 0.9% in the flash estimate. Net exports were stronger than first thought.

Investment in renewable energy remained the largest contributor. Household spending was revised from flat to a small rise.
