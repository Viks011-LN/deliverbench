Title: Kestria: September labour market
Date: 2026-10-16
Desk: Macro

Kestria's unemployment rate rose to 4.8% in September from 4.7% in August. Employment fell slightly in manufacturing and rose in services.

Wage growth slowed to 3.6% year on year, the slowest since early 2023.

The central banks of the region are close to the end of their cycles, but not all at the same point. Wage settlements in the autumn round are coming in below last year's, but well above inflation. The data have been narrow rather than weak, and the quarter so far brought few surprises.
