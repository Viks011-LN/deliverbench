Title: Corvo Telecom: third-quarter preview
Date: 2026-10-13
Desk: Equities

Corvo Telecom reports its third-quarter results on 23 October. Consensus expects third-quarter EBITDA of EUR 488m. A year ago, Corvo Telecom's third-quarter EBITDA margin was 37.1%.

For 2026, Corvo Telecom guides to an EBITDA margin of 37.5% to 38.5% and to capital expenditure of EUR 640m to EUR 680m. Price increases and lower energy costs give it room at the top of the margin range; roaming income over the summer should help too.

Valuations in the sector sit close to their ten-year average, with the discount to the market narrowing. Sentiment toward utilities improved over the quarter so far as investors focused on the outlook for consumer spending. The sector trades at a premium to its history because of the visibility of its earnings. Labour costs remain the biggest unknown for next year's margins.
