Title: Valdoria: September retail sales, first estimate
Date: 2026-10-07
Desk: Macro

Retail sales volumes fell 0.2% month on month in September, according to the first estimate from the statistics office. Clothing and furniture were weak, while food sales held up.

The fall follows two strong months, so we read it as a pause rather than a turn. Online sales are the part of the release most often revised.
