Title: Bellamy Foods: third-quarter preview
Date: 2026-10-20
Desk: Equities

Bellamy Foods publishes its third-quarter trading update on 4 November. Second-quarter revenue was EUR 1,349m, and the company guides to operating profit of EUR 410m to EUR 440m for 2026.

We forecast a cut in the guidance range, to EUR 380m to EUR 410m, as cocoa and packaging costs bite in the second half. The shares already price some of this.

Capital spending plans are rising for the first time in two years. We prefer companies with net cash and pricing power over those that rely on volume growth. Management teams have become more cautious about the second half in their outlook statements.
