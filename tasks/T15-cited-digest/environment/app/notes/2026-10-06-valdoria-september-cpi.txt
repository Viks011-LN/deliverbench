Title: Valdoria: September inflation
Date: 2026-10-06
Desk: Macro

Consumer prices rose 2.9% year on year in September, down from 3.0% in August, as fuel prices fell. Core inflation, which leaves out energy and food, was unchanged at 3.2%.

Services inflation remains the sticking point for the central bank. Goods prices were flat on the month.

Wage settlements in the autumn round are coming in below last year's, but well above inflation. Energy prices remain the largest swing factor for headline inflation over the winter.
