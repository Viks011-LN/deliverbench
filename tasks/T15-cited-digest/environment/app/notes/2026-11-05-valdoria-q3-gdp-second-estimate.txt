Title: Valdoria: third-quarter GDP, second estimate
Date: 2026-11-05
Desk: Macro

Valdoria's third-quarter growth was revised up to 0.7% quarter on quarter in the second estimate. Stronger inventories and a smaller drag from net trade account for the revision.

Household spending was unrevised. The figure strengthens the case for the central bank to keep rates where they are into the new year.
