Title: Norrland Industrial: third-quarter preview
Date: 2026-10-19
Desk: Equities

Norrland Industrial reports on Thursday. Consensus expects third-quarter revenue of EUR 4.15bn, helped by rail deliveries that slipped from the second quarter.

The margin is the number to watch. After an adjusted EBITA margin of 11.6% in the second quarter, the cost programme should start to show; we look for progress rather than a step change.

Order books remain healthy, although lead times have shortened, which points to slower growth next year. The sector trades at a premium to its history because of the visibility of its earnings. Pricing power is the theme of the season: companies that held prices have protected their margins. Capital spending plans are rising for the first time in two years.
