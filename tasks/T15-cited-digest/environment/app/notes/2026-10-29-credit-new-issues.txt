Title: Credit: new issues this week
Date: 2026-10-29
Desk: Credit

Norrland Industrial priced EUR 500m of seven-year bonds at a coupon of 3.125%, the tightest spread for a Valdorian industrial issuer this year. The book was more than three times covered.

Secondary liquidity is adequate, though bid-offer spreads widen quickly on risk-off days. Retail investors have been net buyers of cyclical equities for most of the quarter so far. Demand for longer maturities has been modest, which keeps credit curves steep. Spreads tightened modestly over the quarter so far as new issues were well absorbed. New-issue premiums have compressed to almost nothing for the strongest names.

42 new bond deals were priced over the fortnight, raising EUR 17.2bn. The average new-issue premium was 2bp.
