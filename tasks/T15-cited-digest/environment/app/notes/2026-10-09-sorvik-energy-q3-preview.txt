Title: Sorvik Energy: third-quarter preview
Date: 2026-10-09
Desk: Equities

Sorvik Energy, the Valdorian oil and gas producer, reports its third-quarter results on 2 November. In the second quarter, revenue was EUR 2,960m and the operating margin 15.2%.

The company guides to capital expenditure of EUR 1.8bn to EUR 2.0bn for 2026. With two fields in maintenance over the summer, we expect volumes to dip in the quarter.

Freight rates eased as new ships joined the fleet and port congestion cleared. Long-dated prices have moved far less than prompt prices this year. Crude inventories built for a third week as refinery runs fell into the maintenance season.

Pricing power is the theme of the season: companies that held prices have protected their margins. Share-price reactions to results have rewarded cash flow more than revenue growth. Guidance has been conservative this season, which leaves room for upgrades in the new year.
