Title: Pellmark Retail: second-quarter sales restated
Date: 2026-10-28
Desk: Equities

Pellmark Retail restated its second-quarter sales to EUR 2,575m, from EUR 2,610m, to take out the Kestrian stores it sold on 30 June. The stores are now reported as a discontinued operation.

Nothing else changes: the guidance was set without the Kestrian stores.
