Title: Pellmark Retail: third-quarter trading update
Date: 2026-11-11
Desk: Equities

Pellmark Retail's third-quarter sales were EUR 2,746m, 5.1% more than a year earlier; on a like-for-like basis, sales rose 3.4% year on year.

The company reiterated its guidance for like-for-like sales growth of 2% to 4% in 2026. Gross margin was stable on a year earlier as price investment offset better buying.

Consolidation remains a theme in the sector, though regulators have become stricter. Analysts' estimates for next year still assume a recovery in volumes that the companies do not promise. We prefer companies with net cash and pricing power over those that rely on volume growth.
