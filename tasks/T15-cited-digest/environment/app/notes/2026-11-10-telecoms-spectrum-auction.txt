Title: Telecoms: Valdoria's spectrum auction
Date: 2026-11-10
Desk: Equities

Valdoria's spectrum auction ended on Monday. Corvo Telecom won two blocks of capacity spectrum for EUR 365m, close to the reserve price, and Valdonet one block.

The outcome is good for Corvo Telecom's cash flow: we had assumed it would pay more. Corvus Telecom did not take part.

Pricing power is the theme of the season: companies that held prices have protected their margins. Share-price reactions to results have rewarded cash flow more than revenue growth. Valuations in the sector sit close to their ten-year average, with the discount to the market narrowing.
